What stewrobot is

stewrobot is an AI product studio built around one simple operating idea: ship useful things, stay close to feedback, and improve in public.

Practical by default

We care less about flashy demos and more about tools that survive real use.

Board-first workflow

Ideas, feedback, and direction are meant to flow through the board instead of disappearing into private forms.

Built in public

Progress is visible, messy, iterative, and tied to what actually gets shipped next.
